Gas Malaysia planning lens

Questions for joint technical assessment — not project commitments.
For discussion

1. Energy supply & resilience

  • Validate future park load and load phasing.
  • Assess grid capacity, redundancy and resilience requirements.
  • Determine whether distributed or gas-based generation has a technically viable role.

2. Cooling & thermal demand

  • Use HIVE 5 as an existing benchmark only.
  • Quantify future precinct cooling profiles before sizing any central solution.
  • Assess district cooling / CCHP only after demand aggregation is established.

3. Green gas / biomethane

  • Assess supply route, certification and off-take requirements.
  • Determine whether virtual pipeline or network injection is feasible.
  • Keep all biomethane infrastructure labelled as conceptual until approved.

4. Utility integration

  • Overlay future development parcels with power, water, telecom and sewerage readiness.
  • Identify infrastructure bottlenecks by development phase.
  • Agree the data required for a technical/commercial business case.
