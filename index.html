04 / COLLABORATION

Discussion Opportunities

Potential areas for joint technical assessment after the existing data and infrastructure baseline is established.

01

Energy supply & resilience

Validate load phasing, grid interface, redundancy and the possible role of distributed or gas-based generation.

02

Cogen / CCHP

Assess technical and commercial feasibility for combined electricity and thermal services once demand profiles are confirmed.

03

District cooling

Test whether aggregated future cooling loads justify a centralised precinct or park-level solution.

04

Green gas / biomethane

Assess supply route, gas quality, certification and off-take requirements for any future green-gas application.
